# Failure taxonomy (dev set)

Every failed simulation (reward < 1) gets exactly one mechanical label, assigned by `service_agent.eval.factorial.classify_failure` in a fixed priority order from the simulation record and the offline governance replay. No human judgment, so the same classifier scores every arm. Generated by `uv run python -m service_agent.eval.report_ablation`.

| Failure category | H0 (native) | H1 (gate) | H2 (gate+ledger+recovery) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| max_steps_loop | 7 | 12 | 8 |
| **total failures** | **7** | **12** | **8** |


Note what is *not* here. H0 committed 36 policy-violating writes, yet `policy_violation` does not appear as a failure category -- because most of those violations happened in episodes that still scored reward 1. The model skipped a price confirmation or acted on an unmet precondition and the final database state matched anyway. That is the crux of the safety argument: task reward and policy compliance are different axes, so a violation count that survives successful episodes (the unauthorized-writes column in the ablation report) is a separate, necessary metric. The failures that remain are max-steps loops -- a capability limit of a 4B model, the province of post-training, not of the gate.
